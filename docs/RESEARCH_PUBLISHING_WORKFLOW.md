## Important limits

- Only Shopify's actual main/live role is presented as published. The legacy internal upload machine status is not a trustworthy display label.
- Shopify edit/publication timestamps are detection times, not invented historical event times.
- Existing receipts without a baseline establish one on their first edit check. Earlier edits cannot be reconstructed reliably.
- Read-only file checks do not rewrite Shopify themes. Existing merchant themes, edits and active storefront were not changed.
- Two real read-only checks of an existing unpublished theme tracked 23 files. The first established the legacy baseline; the second correctly remained Uploaded with `edited: false`. No merchant edit was simulated.
- A successful schema check, structural score or render regression is not proof of perceptual design quality.
- The expanded engine no longer chooses finished page families: it independently composes hero geometry, image presentation and each section's layout, surface, heading position, width and spacing. Legacy designs remain supported.
- Verification after the expansion: 71 backend tests, 62 legacy render regressions and seven new composed-render checks passed; API typecheck passed and the application screenshots were inspected.
- Live evidence used the existing mug category and its eight successful captured sources. Four new drafts were obtained after repairing the first batch's fourth concept. Later repeat tests produced one more accepted draft but still could not reliably finish another batch of four, even with explicit closest-layout feedback. No novelty threshold was relaxed and no preset fallback was substituted.
- The mug source has pre-existing unverified variant IDs and missing product facts. These remain disclosed; the local design exercise is not proof of a working real Shopify cart for that source.
- The live creative-generation blocker must be resolved and a fresh research-to-four-design run accepted before declaring this request or the HR assignment complete.

## Content-first generation and honest quality evidence

- A factual campaign brief is built before visual composition. It identifies supported product questions, verified specifications, missing facts and the page's purchase objective. Unknown policies and placeholder values are not turned into claims.
- Campaign composition requires a product story, a factual FAQ and a repeat purchase section; verified specifications require an editable table. At least two substantive explanatory/proof sections are required beyond FAQ and purchase, without introducing fixed whole-page templates.
- FAQ answers come from the saved product/merchant facts, rather than unverified AI paraphrases. Creative narrative and benefit claims still require human factual review and are explicitly marked pending.
- Local checks inspect the generated HTML: headings, metadata, image descriptions, canonical, collection link, Product markup, visible FAQ/markup agreement and visible specification content. Script data alone cannot count as visible facts.
- `SEO`, `AEO` and `GEO` scores are internal coverage, not search rankings or Lighthouse results. Overall readiness is the fraction of local content and campaign checks passed, including unresolved local review items. External tests do not enter this denominator.
- Actual Lighthouse measurements retain their own 85 performance / 95 SEO / 90 accessibility targets. Rich Results, destination-store SEO, actual cart and actual-theme breakpoint verification stay separate and unverified until evidence exists.
- Existing designs are reassessed on reads using their saved product/brand snapshots. This changes their reported checks, not their content, publication receipts or uploaded Shopify files.
- One new content-first campaign draft was generated with the unchanged model against the populated mug category and eight captured references. Its grounded FAQ passed local AEO checks; missing product specifications still prevented a high GEO score. This does not establish reliable repeated four-design batches or external schema certification.
- Final verification: 78 backend tests and 61 frontend tests passed, both artifact typechecks passed, saved designs were reassessed through the real API, and the new draft's standalone theme ZIP downloaded and passed archive checks. Desktop and phone application screenshots were inspected.
- That visual review also exposed generator commentary in the accepted draft's copy. A stricter copy/rationale guard now rejects that class of output; a further bounded live attempt did not produce an accepted replacement. The saved draft was not silently rewritten. Its narrative review remains pending, and repeat-generation reliability is still explicitly incomplete.

### Bowl batch reliability, headless product data and score semantics

Rough planning cost estimates use separate configurable rates, not mini-model rates. Billing is not measured by these estimates.
- The strict planning schema now permits only section kinds supported by actual product facts, images, reviews and finished copy. The 38% structural novelty threshold was not lowered.
- Product analysis reads public Hydrogen purchase-form variant GIDs and inert embedded product JSON; it does not assume every Shopify-backed site implements `/products/handle.js`.
